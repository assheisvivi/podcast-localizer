Use and editorial policies

Terms of use

This is a classroom prototype for short, single-speaker English clips. The producer is responsible for permission to process source audio, checking all facts and translations, and deciding whether any output may be published. Do not upload confidential interviews or material that has not been cleared for third-party processing.

Privacy

The static classroom demo runs in your browser and does not upload its fictional sample. The live Python workflow sends uploaded audio to the configured transcription provider, text to the translation provider, and approved Chinese text to the selected voice provider. The free Edge MP3 option sends the approved Chinese script to Microsoft's online speech service through the edge-tts library; it needs no API key. Source audio is removed after processing. Drafts, editor changes, approvals, and a daily usage count are stored in the service database. Review sessions expire after seven days and expired records are purged when a new session is created. The browser remembers only the review session ID, not the access code or audio. Provider retention practices depend on the provider and configuration; confirm them before using real newsroom material.

AI policy

AI creates a draft transcript, translation, risk flags, and synthetic voice output. Every line requires explicit editor approval before the live service can generate a dub. Flags are triage aids, not fact checks. Disclose synthetic voices to listeners. Do not imitate or clone a person's voice without written consent; voice cloning is outside this MVP.
