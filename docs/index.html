A newsroom workspace for turning English podcast audio into a Chinese dub. AI prepares the transcript and translation, flags risky lines, and stops before synthesis until an editor approves every sentence.

Built for a one-minute newsroom workflowExplore the interactive classroom demo, or use an access code to process your own audio in the hosted review workspace. One speaker and English-to-Mandarin only in this MVP.

Three steps, one editorial gate

Names, numbers, quotes, and terminology stay visible for verification against the source.

01 / PREPARE

Transcribe and translate

Bring in short English audio and create a timestamped Chinese draft.

02 / TRIAGE

Review high-risk lines

See number, glossary, quote, and factual flags at the top of the queue.

03 / APPROVE

Editor sign-off

Correct the text, tag errors, and explicitly approve each segment.

04 / DUB

Generate audio

Only approved text can reach voice synthesis; export the review log.

What the MVP demonstrates
